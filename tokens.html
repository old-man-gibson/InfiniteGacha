Map tokens

Ours are pogs: a head cropped from the LPC frame, a rim that carries state, a name tag. Theirs are sprites at their own size, mirrored when they stand to the left of the party so they turn to face it. The squad moves on the mission clock — one step every 200 ms of wall time, which is one in-game second at 5:1 — and the frame loop interpolates between steps, which is how the real map will run.

Rim: blue fine · amber hurt · red down · gold ward. Sprite height is set by bestiary grade, so a legendary reads as large without a label.
